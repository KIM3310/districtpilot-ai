# Monetization Playbook - districtpilot-ai

This file is a commercial planning artifact, not a guarantee of revenue. Use it to make the repository legible as a sellable product, pilot, workshop, license, or proof-of-value package.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** B2G/B2B district operations analytics
- **Primary buyer:** Local governments, field-service planners, civic analytics teams, and district operators.
- **Revenue wedge:** Forecast-to-action cards for move-in/home-service demand and district resource planning.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Planning dashboard pilot** - pricing hypothesis: $5k-$15k workshop
2. **Forecast review workshop** - pricing hypothesis: $20k-$60k pilot
3. **Monthly operations monitoring setup** - pricing hypothesis: $3k-$12k/month monitoring

## Acquisition Channels

- Public-sector innovation demos
- Snowflake ecosystem storytelling
- District operations one-pager

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- Judge fast path works
- Evidence chain is visible
- Synthetic/real data boundaries are clear

## Risk Boundaries

- Forecasts are decision support
- Public-sector data lineage required
- Human review must stay explicit

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Package 3 district case views
- **60 days:** Create procurement-friendly one-pager
- **90 days:** Offer planning workshop

## Metrics That Matter

- Forecast error
- Action-card adoption
- Resource allocation cycle time

## Positioning Note

Lead with the specific operational pain, then show the runnable proof, then offer a narrow pilot. Do not pitch the repository as a vague app; pitch it as a concrete workflow with evidence, risk boundaries, and a next buying step.
